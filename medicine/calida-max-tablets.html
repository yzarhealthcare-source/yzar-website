About Calida Max Tablets

Calida Max is a dietary food supplement tablet marketed by Yzar Healthcare. It is not for medicinal use.

Composition
  • Calcium Carbonate 500 mg (eq. to elemental Calcium 500 mg)
  • Calcitriol 0.25 mcg
  • Vitamin K2-7 45 mcg
  • Cyanocobalamin 2.2 mcg
  • Magnesium Sulphate 70 mg
  • Vitamin D3 1000 IU
  • Per film coated tablet (approx.), as printed on the pack
How to Use

As prescribed by the dietician.

Warnings & Precautions
  • Dietary food supplement. Not for medicinal use.
  • Keep out of reach of children.
Storage

Store in a cool and dry place. Protect from direct sunlight and moisture.
