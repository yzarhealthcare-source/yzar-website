FAQ

Quick answers

How do I order medicines?

Add medicines to your cart, choose quantity, enter your name and address and tap Order on WhatsApp. Our team will confirm your order.

Do I need a prescription?

Prescription medicines are supplied only against a valid prescription from a registered doctor. Share it with us on WhatsApp.

How is payment done?

Payment and delivery details are confirmed with you on WhatsApp after you place your order.

Can I ask about a medicine before buying?

Yes. Call or message us and we will help. For medical advice, always consult your doctor.
